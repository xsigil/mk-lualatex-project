\begin{definition}[Cybernetic Observation]
Let $\mathcal{D}$ denote the evidence set. The update equation is formulated as:
\begin{equation}
P(\mthSystemOperator \condbar \mathcal{D}) = \frac{P(\mathcal{D} \condbar \mthSystemOperator) P(\mthSystemOperator)}{P(\mathcal{D})}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
